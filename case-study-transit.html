Namma Transit

Role
Product designer
Duration
14 weeks
Tools
Figma, Maze, Sheets
Platform
Android, PWA

Problem

Buying a metro ticket in the app required four screens, a payment redirect, and a stable connection at exactly the moment most riders are underground or on a congested network. Support logs showed the same failure over and over: payment debited, QR code never issued, rider stuck at the gate.

Research method

Two weeks of intercept interviews at three stations — Majestic, Indiranagar, and Yeshwantpur — at peak and off-peak hours, 41 riders total. Each interview paired a five-minute conversation with a timed observation of the rider's next purchase. Alongside this, thirty days of support tickets were coded by failure point.

Findings

Three findings carried the redesign. First, 70% of failed purchases died at the payment redirect, not in the app itself. Second, riders repeat trips: the median rider bought the same two journeys for weeks at a stretch. Third, riders trusted the physical token more than the QR code because the token cannot expire in a tunnel.

The design answer was to make the ticket behave like a token: purchased in advance, stored on the device, valid offline, and shown at the gate without any network call.

Outcome

The offline wallet shipped in March 2026 behind a staged rollout. The numbers at right are the eight-week readings against the previous quarter's baseline.
