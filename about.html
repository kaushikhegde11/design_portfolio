About Me

I am a product designer and photographer working out of Bengaluru. I spent my first years after engineering college building interfaces for logistics software, which taught me that the most important screen is usually the one a tired person sees at the worst possible moment. I have designed for that person ever since.

The photography is not a hobby bolted onto the portfolio; it is where the design habits come from. Walking with a camera trains you to notice how people actually move through a market, a platform, a queue — and product work is mostly queues. When a project involves field research, I bring the camera and the discount it buys you on people's patience.

I work best on problems with physical consequences: transit, small commerce, tools for other makers. I am slow to add features and quick to remove steps.

Service history

  • 2026 Independent practice. Namma Transit offline ticketing shipped in March.
  • 2024 Fieldnotes, a capture tool for film photographers, released as a side project; 3,000 users in the first year.
  • 2022 Senior product designer, logistics SaaS. Led the driver-app redesign across 11 languages.
  • 2019 First design role, after two years as a front-end developer. Kept the habit of shipping my own CSS.
